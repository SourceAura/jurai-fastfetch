Insei-fastfetch: provenance

Designed by 0xSrcAura in 2026, first for the Jurai suite;
it is Insei's now, kept and updated as part of her. The
voice, the colours (the house prism, cyan #22D3EE to
violet #A78BFA, with the Kasugai priority colours for
percents), the emblem (a faceted prism tree under a broken
spectrum ring, the seal 樹 pressed at its corner; original
artwork created for this preset), and the identity line
are original work. The structure follows the fastfetch
preset grammar. No Ryoku Team assets.

Jurai Fastfetch: provenance

Designed by 0xSrcAura for the Jurai suite (2026). The voice,
the colours (the house prism, cyan #22D3EE to violet
#A78BFA, with the Kasugai priority colours for percents),
the emblem (a faceted prism tree under a broken spectrum
ring, the seal 樹 pressed at its corner; original artwork
created for this preset), and the identity line are
original to Jurai. The structure follows the fastfetch
preset grammar shared by the whole shelf. No Ryoku Team
assets.
